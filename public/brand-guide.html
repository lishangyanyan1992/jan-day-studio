02 · Identity

The marks.

The supplied logo artwork is always the source of truth. Keep its proportions, color, and letterforms intact.

Primary wordmarkMin. 120 px
Secondary markMin. 32 px

Give it room

Keep at least the height of the lowercase “a” clear on every side.

Use the artwork

Never typeset, redraw, stretch, outline, shadow, or crop the logo.

Choose contrast

Place it on Canvas, Ivory, Ink, or calm imagery with strong contrast.

07 · Guidance

Keep it
recognizably Jan Day.

Do

  • Use the supplied logo artwork unchanged.
  • Let clay, typography, and negative space build recognition.
  • Make pickup, delivery, and sourcing easy to find.
  • Show warm, natural, lived-in photography.

Do not

  • Recreate the logo with live type.
  • Add gold gradients, scripts, florals, or drop shadows.
  • Use bright blush pink as the dominant color.
  • Round every card or bury useful information.

Accessibility is part of the brand.

Meet WCAG AA contrast, keep visible keyboard focus, preserve semantic headings and labels, maintain 44 px touch targets, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
