## Shop `/shop`

**Headline:** Good things, carefully picked.

**Browse note:** Jan Day is browse-only online for now. The assortment will change often, so prices shown here are typical ranges—not a promise of current stock.

1. **Lifestyle gifts · $5–$30.** Cheerful, useful, and just a little unexpected. Look for stationery, plush, lucky cats, keychains, phone charms, and small desk companions.
2. **Teaware & tableware · $8–$45.** Quietly beautiful pieces for tea, dinner, and the everyday table: teacups, small teapots, chopstick sets, bowls, and sculptural chopstick rests.
3. **Seasonal & festival · $3–$40.** Small traditions and festive details for Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn Festival, and the moments our community celebrates together.
4. **Home & decor · $10–$50.** Ceramics, Chinese knot charms, folding fans, and small prints chosen to add character without taking over the room.
5. **Jan Day Originals · $5–$35.** Our signature line of illustrated cards, tote bags, prints, and gift boxes—designed by Jan Day and made to be kept.

**How shopping works:** Come browse in person during China Wok's hours. We'll share new arrivals on Instagram and are happy to answer a quick stock question before you make a special trip.

## Jan Day Originals `/originals`

**Headline:** Made by Jan Day. Made to be given.

**Introduction:** Original illustration, useful formats, and color that feels like us. Designed in-house in small runs.

**Manifesto:** Not a souvenir. A little piece of the shop. Jan Day Originals are the heart of the collection: work we can't find anywhere else because we made it ourselves. The line will grow with the pilot—one card, print, and useful object at a time.

- **Illustrated cards · $5–$9.** Small notes with a point of view—made for birthdays, thank-yous, celebrations, and just because.
- **Tote bags · $18–$28.** Useful canvas carryalls with original Jan Day artwork, made for market runs and library stacks.
- **Small prints · $12–$25.** Colorful, easy-to-frame artwork for dorm rooms, reading corners, and thoughtful walls.
- **Gift boxes · $20–$35.** A ready-to-give mix of paper goods and small finds, wrapped with the Jan Day touch.

**Product promise:** Drawn with warmth. Printed with texture. Easy to live with.
